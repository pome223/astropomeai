# Recordings and figures

The initial collection contains unchanged public MissionOS experiment assets, selected for the corresponding research articles. Public-repository entries in `provenance.json` record the pinned source URL, Git blob hash, SHA-256 digest, byte count, dimensions, and video duration/codec where applicable. Some existing figures and posters are reused from `assets/figures/`.

The source is public MissionOS commit `7f0128491ac1e998c05580b50707ee8cab7543c6`. The [original Apache-2.0 license](../figures/MISSIONOS-LICENSE.txt) is retained. Modified Yokohama City / Project PLATEAU scene data are attributed in the articles and the [source attribution record](https://github.com/pome223/missionos/blob/7f0128491ac1e998c05580b50707ee8cab7543c6/docs/examples/yokohama-urban-scene/ATTRIBUTION.md), under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

These files are saved simulations, forecasts, or measured-result figures as identified by each caption. They are not newly executed experiments. Video duration, simulator elapsed time, and wall time are distinct. The videos do not autoplay and are loaded only when the reader starts playback.

The Starship video and poster added on 24 October are unchanged copies of a reviewed saved-record export supplied for publication. Their source kind and hashes are recorded separately; see the [portable evidence summary](../../content/evidence/starship-october-record-update.json). The six-second tower scene is illustrative, no catch was performed, and this presentation does not issue a return certificate.
