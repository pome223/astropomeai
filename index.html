About this collection

A result is only as useful
as its evidence.

Astropome AI is an independent research project exploring governed AI, autonomous systems, and computational biology. These articles bring together the question, the method, the measured result, and the limits of each study.

The MissionOS articles are based on a pinned snapshot of the public main branch. The biology articles introduce curated summaries of saved computational studies, with links to their published biological context and supplementary evidence.

These are technical research articles, not peer-reviewed publications. Simulator outcomes, synthetic data, and empirical observations are identified within each article. Negative results remain part of the record.
